Group discussion – imagining the future organization

Three groups explore how AI changes customers, innovation and employees – first by generating ideas, then clustering and prioritising them into a frontier action plan.
⏱ 20 minutes per stage (facilitator can adapt)
Customer group
Customer relationships
Explore how AI can deepen relationships with customers by creating adaptive, personalised and proactive experiences.
Brainstorm question
Where does our engagement feel reactive, impersonal or inconsistent – and what could AI help us do differently?
Inbound support is mostly firefighting – AI triages intent and predicts high-risk cases before they escalate.
Example idea Signal-based outreach
Customers get generic campaigns – AI builds next-best action journeys tailored to behaviour, value and lifecycle.
Example idea Adaptive journeys
Each idea should be concrete and observable in 12 months.
Innovation group
Experimentation & value
Discover how to accelerate experimentation and value creation by embedding AI into the ways ideas form, evolve and scale.
Brainstorm question
It’s one year from now. What’s different about how we work, serve customers and innovate with AI?
Every quarter we run an AI “product studio” – small teams prototype, test and either scale or retire ideas quickly.
Example idea AI Product Studio
New ideas are shaped with AI co-design – copilots draft journeys, KPIs and technical options for teams to debate.
Example idea AI-assisted design
Focus on how ideas start, evolve and get tested.
Employee group
Work & culture
Reimagine how AI transforms work itself – empowering employees, enhancing decisions and removing everyday friction.
Brainstorm question
It’s one year from now. How do our people grow and feel part of something bigger because of AI?
Every employee has a personal Copilot that supports learning plans, career moves and feedback on real work.
Example idea Growth copilots
Teams use AI to remove low-value admin, freeing time for coaching, problem-solving and connecting with customers.
Example idea Friction-free work
Think about skills, autonomy and belonging.
Customer group
Clustering & prioritising
Of all the moments we could transform, which would build the strongest loyalty or trust?
Predictive outreach for at-risk customers; AI flags churn signals and prompts proactive actions.
Cluster: Proactive care Priority: P1
AI-guided service journeys with consistent answers across chat, phone and field.
Cluster: Consistency Priority: P2
Innovation group
Clustering & prioritising
Which actions could deliver quick learning and visible proof of change – and which lay foundations for long-term transformation?
Launch an AI Product Studio with 3 sprints per year, each surfacing 3–4 experiments.
Cluster: Experimentation engine Priority: P1
Build a shared pattern library for successful AI use cases and architectures.
Cluster: Reusable patterns Priority: P2
Employee group
Clustering & prioritising
If we could only bring a few of these stories to life, which would create the biggest difference for our people and culture?
Organisation-wide rollout of AI-assisted onboarding and learning journeys.
Cluster: Growth & skills Priority: P1
AI-supported workload balancing and wellbeing insights for managers.
Cluster: Wellbeing & sustainability Priority: P2
